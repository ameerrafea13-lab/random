About Breakdoor

Enter a mysterious and eerie facility filled with locked doors and unsettling secrets. Breakdoor is a puzzle-solving horror game where you must break through doors and escape mysterious rooms while uncovering the truth.

Game Features

  • Solve complex puzzles to progress
  • Break through locked doors with various methods
  • Explore haunting environments
  • Uncover mysterious storyline
  • Find clues and hidden items
  • Multiple endings based on your choices
  • Atmospheric horror gameplay

Puzzle Tips

  • Examine all objects carefully for clues
  • Look for patterns and codes
  • Try combining items in different ways
  • Pay attention to environmental details
  • Read all notes and documents thoroughly
  • Some puzzles have multiple solutions
