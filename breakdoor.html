About BreakDoor

BreakDoor is an asymmetrical Roblox survival game. Survivors defend their numbered safe rooms until rescue arrives, while Hunters move between rooms and try to break through every defense before time runs out.

Survivor objective

  • Build up a personal safe room and survive until the rescue helicopter arrives.
  • Upgrade the Bank to improve Cash and Gold production.
  • Strengthen the door and turret as Hunter pressure increases.
  • Use the Laboratory to unlock weapon, vision, poison, and character upgrades.
  • Watch for door warnings and support teammates when Hunters target their rooms.

Hunter objective

  • Move between Survivor rooms, choose vulnerable targets, and break their doors.
  • Work with other Hunters to pressure defenders before rescue.
  • Grow stronger during the round through Hunter levels and Hunter Core upgrades.
  • Adapt targets as Survivors improve their doors, turrets, and research.

Core systems

  • Economy: Banks and production upgrades create the Cash and Gold needed for stronger defenses.
  • Doors: Door upgrades increase a room's ability to hold back Hunters.
  • Turrets: Upgrade damage, range, and fire rate to defend the room automatically.
  • Laboratory: Research improves weapons and other survival tools.

Starter priorities

  • Build economy early so later upgrades remain affordable.
  • Balance door strength with turret damage instead of spending everything on one system.
  • React quickly when Hunters focus a teammate's room.
  • Remember: BreakDoor is separate from DOORS and has its own survivor-versus-hunter gameplay.
