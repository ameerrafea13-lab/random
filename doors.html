What is DOORS?

DOORS is a multiplayer Roblox horror game developed by LSPLASH and RediblesQW. Players travel through rooms by opening numbered doors, searching drawers and containers for useful items, solving puzzles, and surviving entities that can appear without warning.

Every run is different. Listen carefully for sounds, watch for visual warnings such as flickering lights, and work together when playing with friends.

Floors and Areas

The Hotel

The first main floor. It includes areas such as the Reception, Library, Courtyard, Greenhouse, and Electrical Room.

The Mines

The second main floor. Players explore darker underground areas including caves, bridges, sewers, a railway station, and the Nest.

The Backdoor

A fast-paced subfloor with a timer. It introduces threats such as Blitz, Haste, and Lookman.

The Rooms

A secret office-like subfloor accessed from The Hotel. It has hundreds of rooms and special entities such as A-60, A-90, and A-120.

Important Entities

Rush

Flickering lights usually warn that Rush is coming. Quickly hide in a wardrobe, closet, or other safe hiding spot.

Ambush

Ambush can travel through rooms more than once. Leave your hiding spot after it passes, then hide again if it returns.

Seek

During a chase sequence, run forward and follow the correct path. Watch for guiding visual effects that show the way.

Figure

Figure cannot see, but it can hear players. Crouch, move carefully, and avoid making noise when it is nearby.

Screech

When you hear its “psst” sound in a dark room, look around quickly to find Screech before it attacks.

Eyes

Do not look directly at Eyes. Turn your camera away and keep moving through the room.

Hide

Hide appears if you stay in a hiding place for too long. Leave when it is safe, especially after Rush has passed.

Dupe

Dupe can appear behind a wrong numbered door. Remember the last door number to avoid choosing the incorrect one.

Useful Items

  • Keys: Open locked doors.
  • Lockpicks: Open certain locked doors and containers.
  • Lighter: Helps you see in dark rooms.
  • Flashlight: A longer-lasting light source.
  • Vitamins: Give a temporary speed boost.
  • Crucifix: Can temporarily restrain some entities.

Beginner Survival Tips

  1. Always listen for sounds before opening the next door.
  2. Check drawers and containers for keys, coins, and items.
  3. Remember where nearby hiding spots are located.
  4. Do not rush through dark rooms without a light source.
  5. In multiplayer, stay close enough to your team.
  6. Use every death to learn an entity’s warning signs.
